How to contact me

Please email me directly at martin.takac@mbzuai.ac.ae. Generic messages are hard to evaluate. Your email should be specific about the research you want to do with me and why my group is the right place for it.

Please include

  • The research direction or project you want to work on: optimization, AI for science, AI for energy, LLMs, or a specific project from my papers.
  • A short description of the concrete problem you want to study, not just a broad area.
  • Why your background is a good fit, including relevant papers, code, math, systems, or domain experience.
  • Your CV, transcript if you are a student, and links to papers, code, or other work that best represent you.
