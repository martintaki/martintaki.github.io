Research direction

AI for Energy

Domain-specific AI for energy workflows, including LLMs and VLMs for causal, mathematical, and physics-grounded reasoning.

EnergyAI Domain LLMs Physics-grounded reasoning Subsurface workflows

Focus

This direction builds AI systems for exploration, production, and subsurface workflows where the model must reason with text, images, physics, uncertainty, and operational constraints.

Typical Questions

  • How can domain LLMs and VLMs reason with physical and mathematical constraints?
  • How can AI support causal and uncertainty-aware decisions in energy workflows?
  • How should multimodal models combine reports, diagrams, sensor data, and expert knowledge?
  • How can we evaluate reliability in specialized, high-stakes technical domains?
