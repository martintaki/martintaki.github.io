Research direction

Optimization for Machine Learning

Algorithms and theory for scalable learning systems, with emphasis on stochastic, distributed, adaptive, and second-order methods.

Stochastic optimization Distributed training Second-order methods Large-scale ML

Focus

This direction studies optimization methods that make modern machine learning more efficient, reliable, and theoretically grounded. The work spans randomized algorithms, variance reduction, distributed methods, communication-efficient training, and curvature-aware algorithms.

Typical Questions

  • How can we train large models faster under realistic communication and memory constraints?
  • When do adaptive or second-order methods provide meaningful advantages?
  • How should stochastic algorithms be designed when gradients, constraints, or objectives are noisy?
  • Can we build algorithms with both practical speed and clean mathematical guarantees?

Methods
