Research direction

AI for Science

Machine learning systems for scientific discovery, structured prediction, causal reasoning, and domain-constrained modeling.

Scientific discovery Biology Causal modeling Structured prediction

Focus

This direction develops AI methods that respect scientific structure rather than treating scientific data as generic prediction problems. The work connects optimization, representation learning, causal reasoning, and domain knowledge.

Typical Questions

  • How can models incorporate constraints, mechanisms, and scientific prior knowledge?
  • How can AI help with discovery when data is expensive, noisy, or incomplete?
  • How do we make predictions interpretable enough to support scientific reasoning?
  • Which optimization methods best support structured scientific models?
